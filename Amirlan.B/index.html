CROCHET

Crocheting is a popular textile craft that uses a single hooked
needle (a crochet hook) to interlock loops of yarn, thread, or fabric. Unlike knitting,
which keeps many live stitches open on needles at once, crochet finishes each stitch
individually before moving to the next.

WHAT WILL YOU NEED?

  • Crochet hook
  • Yarn
  • Stitch marker
  • Scissors
  • Needle
  • Thread
